SWEET WORLD GENERATOR 1.7: CAVERN OASES
=====================================

Requires Harmony and Anomaly. Odyssey is optional and adds native fishing.
Use a NEW colony. This mode does not convert an existing surface colony.

STARTING
Choose Cavern Pioneers or Reclamation Expedition on the scenario screen.
Both automatically select the strict Cavern World recipe while keeping the
other world controls available. The recipe alone is also in world controls.
Native water tiles remain water on the travel grid. Use land for a colony.
Use 250 by 250 colony maps for the intended cavern experience. Smaller and
larger maps remain compatibility options, starting at 100 cells per side.

Cavern Pioneers: three settlers stand inside a safe oasis, with 70 survival
meals, 20 medicine, 450 steel, 300 wood, 30 components, two bolt-action rifles,
a steel knife and 400 silver. Equip the supplied weapons normally.
New peaceful player starts also receive an adult female and male muffalo.
Build a pen and keep grazing ground available; they use native animal care.

Reclamation Expedition: four explorers stand in the outer caves near an oasis
guarded by three actual insect-faction megascarabs. Each explorer has a
bolt-action rifle equipped. Supplies: 100 survival meals, 25 medicine,
350 steel, 250 wood, 25 components and 300 silver. Starting balance is
provisional, not a promise that every randomly generated team will win.

THE CAVERNS
The normal world grid is the travel abstraction. Ordinary local colony and
faction maps on cavern tiles use persistent underground generation. There is
no separate surface map or entrance from above. Each local map initially has
one primary fertile lake oasis, connected through a four-cell-wide passage
to an outer cave network reaching the map edges. Other world tiles provide
other oases to explore. Natural walls remain mineable, including new routes
made after generation. The deep natural ceiling cannot collapse.

New cavern maps have asymmetric natural-edged chambers, uneven off-centre
lakes and branching tunnels of varying widths, with occasional larger caves.
The outer map boundary remains rectangular. Layout randomness is seeded and
validated for connected routes, dry arrivals and usable farmland. Existing
saved maps keep their original terrain and are not reshaped by this update.

The mode does not use the temporary Undercave lifecycle, forced Dreadmeld,
flesh-sack generation or mandatory spreading-flesh generators. The outer
floor is flesh-themed terrain. This is not immunity from ordinary Anomaly
storyteller threats. Specialized objective maps, such as the escape ship,
remain native exceptions instead of having their objectives overwritten.

HABITAT
The oasis has rich, heavy-building-capable dry ground and a real lake.
Cave grass needs no sunlight. Light-tolerant lantern fungi produce edible
raw fungus and can be sown on ground with at least 100% fertility. Natural
crystal clusters and fungi illuminate the generated grassy habitat;
outer tunnels have dark stretches, faintly glowing violet Hair, lava pockets
and player lights. Ordinary crops still
need enough light, temperature and fertility. Use the bright habitat patches
or build appropriate growing equipment. Clearing a crystal removes its light.

Broad-canopied cavern oaks grow without sunlight and yield ordinary wood.
Research Tree sowing to cultivate them. Cavern healroot also grows in darkness
and produces herbal medicine; sowing needs Plants skill 8. Slow seedling
regrowth respects growing zones and structures, and starts only on new maps.

Fine dark-violet Hair grows over exposed Flesh in new cavern maps. Its
strands are seventy percent narrower than ordinary cave grass and emit a
small purple glow. Hair is groundcover, not animal forage or fertile soil.
Mining Flesh-backed walls preserves the Flesh and deep ceiling. After loose
mining debris is cleared, Hair can slowly colonize the new ground. Pending
growth survives saving; floors, growing zones and structures suppress it.
Paving or reserving an established patch clears it during a plant update.
Legacy maps retain their earlier ecology and mining behavior.

Four wild hares begin in the dry oasis. Wildlife can roam, graze, be tamed
or hunted normally. Reclamation and faction oases contain a wild adult
female/male muffalo pair; tame them for native livestock breeding and wool.
Peaceful new player starts receive a domestic pair instead. Wildlife is not
an inexhaustible spawner. Habitat regrowth does not regenerate crystals.
With Odyssey, the lake uses native fish populations and fishing zones/jobs.
Fish are resources caught through the fishing system, not decorative pawns.
Without Odyssey, the lake remains usable water with no fishing dependency.

Natural cavern walls use the tile's rock types and contain mineable steel,
components and smaller valuable mineral deposits accessible from tunnels.
A dry oasis geyser supports geothermal power. Wood supports fuel production;
solar power cannot work under this deep ceiling. Ground trade supplements
finite resources. Better Trees and Better Mountains remain optional visual
providers; neither is needed for cavern tree growth or the rock/resource fill.

Molten pools sit deeper inside broad side chambers, at least seven tiles
from the original travel network. Each chamber meets that network through
one opening on the same side, with broad dry margins around the lava. The
chambers do not replace original routes or fertile ground. Keep animals and
combustible materials away from their burning edges. Odyssey uses native
deep lava; without Odyssey, cavern magma uses the same terrain heat, light,
burning and impassable-ground mechanics with a simpler native-texture look.
Native room-temperature rules apply: caves connected to map edges use outdoor
temperature, while enclosed rooms can retain terrain heat from lava. Keep
livestock and combustible supplies away from any lava enclosure.

EXPLORATION AND FACTIONS
Normal configured human factions can own cavern maps with their native pawn
types.
Human faction sites receive compact furnished stone lodges; insect-held
oases have an occupied ruined structure. Layouts stay on dry habitat, clear
of the passage, water and arrivals. Custom Base Framework can stamp the
original bounded layouts when loaded; native generation supplies them when
the optional framework is absent. Full city generation is not run here.

Up to two insect-held oasis locations are added to a new cavern
colony's world when the native insect faction exists. Each must be a valid
settlement tile with a ground route from the starting colony. If the world
has fewer suitable locations, a message reports the actual number placed;
inaccessible markers are not substituted. Insects have real faction and
defend-point AI. Clearing one oasis does not eliminate the universal insect
faction. Later map loads do not rerun initialization or respawn defenders.

Ordinary infestation incidents and deep-drill infestation incidents are
disabled specifically on these cavern maps. Deliberately placed insect
factions remain. Surface incidents and other Anomaly threats are unchanged.

Form caravans and use the ordinary world-map travel/attack/settle controls.
An explorer enters through a map-edge tunnel and can reach the oasis through
its passage. Clear the defenders before using the native settle action.
To establish additional colonies, increase RimWorld's normal maximum-colonies
setting yourself. The mod does not change that preference or your mod list.

TRADE AND SKY ACCESS
Ground trading caravans are supported, including pack animals and a reachable
gathering point. Orbital trades are deliberately unavailable in these deep
caverns because their drop deliveries cannot reach the colony. The comms
guard explains this instead of opening a trade payment dialog. Normal surface
orbital trading is unchanged. Surface airships, gravship starts, orbital cargo
and other mods' sky transport are not certified for these roofed maps. Use
the included standing-arrival scenarios for the supported cavern starts.

SAVES, LIMITATIONS AND ART
Back up colonies before updating. Custom biome, floor, ceiling, plant,
crystal, faction/scenario components are saved content. Removing this mod,
downgrading, or opening these colonies in 1.6 is not supported. The legacy
1.6 payload itself remains unchanged. No player saves are converted.

Native tests exercise bounded map sizes, starts, gameplay consumers and
save/load. They do not certify every seed, full modpack, faction provider,
long-term colony balance or every storyteller/quest/transport combination.
Static biome reference pictures are a separate feature, not a live preview.

OasisCrystal.png and UGWG_HairViolet.png are original artwork generated for
this project using the built-in image generator. The Hair sprite contains
its purple colour directly. Native grass/fungus art is referenced through
RimWorld's content loader; no official or third-party texture file was copied.
Existing package attribution, licensing and technical identity are retained.
