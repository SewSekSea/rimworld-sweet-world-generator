SWEET WORLD GENERATOR - RIMWORLD 1.7 COMPATIBILITY

This release's expanded cavern features target RimWorld 1.7.4982. The separate
1.6 folder is preserved legacy support, not the new cavern implementation.
Harmony is required. Anomaly is required for Cavern World. Odyssey is optional
and supplies native deep lava and lake fishing; a magma fallback is available.

ReGrowth 2: native tests used installed version 5.2-rev2, package
regrowth.botr.core, Workshop 2260097569. Five accepted native cases include
three with Alpha Biomes also loaded. This certifies only the exercised cases.

Alpha Biomes: can coexist with Cavern World while the cavern biome is retained.
It does not automatically replace cavern tiles. Forced biome conversion or
retrofitting existing maps is outside the tested setup. A separate Alpha-only
Gallatross graveyard surface map with a derelict archonexus mutator logged
three reload conflicts involving overlapping bone buildings and indestructible
archotech structures. The identical map reproduced those errors with Sweet
World Generator absent. That case is excluded from the accepted zero-error
suite; it is a known uncertified Alpha surface combination. The accepted
Alpha surface sample used Mycotic Jungle.

Custom Base Framework is optional. Its bounded original layouts have native
structure fallbacks. This mod does not include an entire city-generation mod.

The accepted suite comprises 46 native cases and 1,467 assertions with zero
game errors/protected-file changes. Additional screenshot sessions are recorded
separately. This is not certification of every seed, modpack, faction provider,
long-term campaign, quest or storyteller combination.

Cavern starts: use the included Cavern Pioneers or Reclamation Expedition
scenarios and 250 x 250 maps. Ground traders are supported. Orbital deliveries
cannot reach the deep ceiling. Gravship starts, surface airships and other
mods' sky transport are not certified. Special objective maps remain native.
Lava is recessed away from original paths; enclosed-room heat still applies.

Start a new colony for expanded generation. Existing maps keep their terrain.
Back up saves. Removing/downgrading the mod or opening cavern saves in 1.6 is
unsupported. Player saves are not converted. Existing attribution, licensing,
technical identity and all legacy 1.6 files remain unchanged. Original crystal,
Hair and promotional thumbnail artwork used the built-in image generator.
